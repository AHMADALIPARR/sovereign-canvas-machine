# ADR 0002 — Artin equivalence for three strands

Braid equality is the Artin action on the free group F3, freely reduced.
The representation is faithful for the braid group, so identical invariants
are equality in B3 and not merely the underlying permutation. The
permutation length is only a lower bound and cannot authorize a proposal.
Quantum equality is Clifford tableau equality up to global phase.
Recurrence equality is exact integer recomputation inside the published bound.
